Welcome! I'm Anna Martin-Boyle, a Ph.D. candidate in computer science at the University of Minnesota. I am a member of the [GroupLens Research Lab](https://grouplens.org/), where I am advised by [Dr. Harmanpreet Kaur](https://harmanpk.github.io/). My research focuses on human-AI collaboration in scholarly information systems, with an emphasis on how LLM-augmented interfaces can help researchers find and make sense of large volumes of scientific knowledge. I study provenance, trust, and reliance in LLM-based scholarly question-answering systems, and develop expert-centered approaches for evaluating LLM reliability in domain-specific contexts.
